## FAQ

**Can I try before I buy?**

**What payment methods do you accept?**
> Credit card, ACH, and wire transfer for Enterprise customers.

**Can I upgrade or downgrade anytime?**
> Yes, you can change plans at any time. Changes take effect on your next billing cycle.

**What happens if I exceed my limits?**
> We'll notify you and offer options to upgrade or purchase add-ons
