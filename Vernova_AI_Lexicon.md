# The Vernova AI Lexicon (v1.0)
*Operationalizing Engram Concepts for the Advanced Research Center*

**Preface:**
Standard AI focuses on "Generation." Industrial AI must focus on "Provenance" and "Context." To operationalize this at the ARC, we are extending the standard AI Periodic Table with a new series of elements: **The Context Series.**

## **Group I: The "Context Series" (The Engram Elements)**

### **Ec - Context Ecology**
* **Definition:** The shift from viewing data as static files (Data Lakes) to viewing it as a living environment where information has a lifecycle, relationships, and "decay" rates.
* **Vernova Application:** A turbine's maintenance log isn't just a PDF; it is part of an ecology that includes the weather data from that day and the grid load at that second.

### **Em - Emergent AI**
* **Definition:** Intelligence that arises from the interaction of specialized agents within a shared context, rather than from explicit top-down programming.
* **Vernova Application:** Instead of writing a rule for "If Grid Overload > X," we create agents for "Safety," "Load," and "Supply" that negotiate the best outcome in real-time.

### **Kv - Keyword Vector Graph**
* **Definition:** The proprietary "triad" structure (Entity-Action-Context) used to ingest vast document corpuses while retaining semantic precision.
* **Vernova Application:** The mechanism we use to ingest 50 years of GE engineering manuals so an AI can cite the specific page of a 1985 safety protocol.

### **Mp - Memory Provenance**
* **Definition:** The auditable trail of *exactly* which piece of memory influenced an AI's decision.
* **Vernova Application:** Critical for safety compliance. If the AI suggests shutting down a sector, we need to know it did so because of "Sensor A" and "Protocol B," not a hallucination.

## **Group II: The "Industrial State" (The Goals)**

### **Vr - Vector Resonance**
* **Definition:** The strength of the signal when a current operational anomaly matches a historical pattern in the Keyword Vector Graph.
* **Vernova Application:** "This vibration pattern in the wind turbine has high *Vector Resonance* with the 2018 blade failure event."

### **Mw - The Memory Wall**
* **Definition:** The theoretical limit where standard LLMs fail because they cannot process enough context to make a safe decision.
* **Vernova Application:** The specific barrier Engram is designed to break, allowing us to feed terabytes of sensor data into a decision window.
